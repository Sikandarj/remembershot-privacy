# RememberShot Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 9 October 2026**

RememberShot helps you turn a photo, screenshot, or image you share into a reminder. The app is designed to keep your image and reminder content on your device.

## Information the app uses

- **Photos and camera images:** An image you select, capture, or share is read on your device. If you save a reminder, the app keeps a copy of that image in its private app storage until you delete the reminder or uninstall the app.
- **Recognized text and reminder details:** Text recognition and date/context extraction happen on your device. If you save a reminder, its title, date, time, category, notes, source image path, and recognized text are stored in the app's local database.
- **Notifications:** If you allow notifications, RememberShot schedules reminder notifications locally on your device.
- **Pro purchase:** Optional Pro purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. RememberShot receives the purchase status needed to enable Pro. Payment details are handled by Google Play and are not collected by RememberShot.

## Where data is stored and sent

RememberShot does not operate an account system, backend, cloud sync, advertising, or analytics service. It does not upload images, recognized text, or reminder content. App backup and device-transfer backup are disabled. Removing the app also removes its locally stored reminders and image copies.

Google Play handles purchase transactions under Google's own terms and privacy disclosures. The Android operating system and Google Play services may process information as part of providing their platform features; consult their privacy information for details.

## Permissions

- **Camera:** used only when you choose to take a photo.
- **Notifications:** used only for local reminder alerts, after you choose to enable them.
- **Vibration and boot completion:** used to provide notification feedback and restore scheduled reminders after a device restart.

The app does not request microphone, contacts, location, or broad photo-library/storage access. Image selection uses Android's system photo picker.

## Retention and deletion

Saved reminder details and their image copies remain on your device until you delete a reminder or uninstall RememberShot. Google Play retains transaction records according to its own policies.

## Children

RememberShot is a general-purpose utility and is not designed specifically for children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
